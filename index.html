What Changed

Teek used to be a fully offline tool app. We've now added a social platform — sign-up, profiles, posts, comments, messages, followers — which means some data is stored on our servers. This policy explains exactly what. The offline tools (notes, recipes, courses, media player, file manager, etc.) still run entirely on your device and never send data to us.

Overview

Teek is an app with two parts: (1) a social platform where users can register, create profiles, post content, comment, message, and follow each other — and (2) a set of offline tools (notes, courses, recipes, scripts, business hub, media player, file manager, code preview, etc.) that run entirely on your device.

This policy explains what data we collect for each part. Sections 3–6 describe the social platform. Section 7 describes the offline tools, which are private by design.

2

Data We Collect

When you register an account and use the social features of Teek, we collect:

  • Account data — email, password (hashed), username, phone number (optional), first and last name
  • Profile data — profile photo, cover photo, bio, category (e.g. "Tipster"), social media links you choose to add
  • Content you create — posts (text, images, videos), comments, likes, bookmarks, shares, stories
  • Messages — direct messages you send and receive
  • Relationships — who you follow and who follows you
  • Notifications — alerts you received or that were sent to you
  • Device & security — device model, brand, OS version, app activity on the device, IP address, login timestamps
  • Preferences — settings, last activity, account status

You can choose not to provide optional data (phone, social links, bio), but the social platform requires at minimum an email, password, and username.

3

Account & Authentication

To sign in, you provide an email (or username or phone) and a password. We store a hashed version of your password using industry-standard hashing — we never store the plaintext password.

When you log in successfully, we issue you an authentication token that is stored on your device and sent with each request to prove who you are. Tokens expire automatically and can be revoked when you log out or delete your account.

For security, when you sign in from a new device we send you an email notification with the device name and location (based on IP). This is a safety feature, not marketing.

4

Social Data

Everything you post publicly — posts, comments, likes, shares, followers, your profile — is visible to other users of Teek. Do not post anything you want to keep private.

When you delete a post or comment, it is removed from public view immediately and permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days (unless you also delete your account, in which case the retention rules in Section 15 apply).

Your email, phone number, and password are never shown to other users. Only your username, name, bio, photo, category, and social links are public.

5

Messages

Direct messages you send and receive are stored on our servers so both you and the recipient can view them. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. They are transmitted over HTTPS (encrypted in transit) and stored in our database.

We do not read your messages except when legally compelled or when investigating a report of abuse in accordance with Section 18.

6

Device & Login Information

For security and multi-device session management, when you log in we collect:

  • Device model, brand, and OS version
  • IP address (recorded at login)
  • Login timestamp
  • A device identifier used only to detect new-device logins

This data is used to notify you of new logins and to protect your account. It is not used for advertising.

7

Offline Tools

The offline tools in Teek — notes, courses, recipes, scripts, code preview, legal drafting, business hub, file manager, music player, video browser, photo gallery, global search, reading time, drafts, PIN lock — operate entirely on your device. Data you create in these tools is stored in your device's private SQLite database and is never transmitted to our servers.

We cannot see what you write in these tools. If you uninstall the app, this data is gone permanently because we never had a copy.

8

Media & Files

Content you upload to the social platform (post images, videos, profile photos, cover photos, stories) is stored on our servers so other users can see it. We keep it until you delete the post/profile or delete your account.

Media and files in the offline tools (photos you browse, videos you watch, music you import, files in the file manager) are accessed read-only or copied into the app's private storage on your device. They never leave your device.

9

Wallet & Payments

If you use wallet top-up, withdrawals, subscriptions, or shop features, we store:

  • Wallet balance and transaction history
  • Bank name, account name, and account number (only the ones you provide for withdrawals)
  • Reference IDs from our payment processor(s)

We do not store your card details or bank credentials. All card and bank processing is handled by licensed third-party payment providers (e.g. Flutterwave). We only receive a confirmation of successful payment and a reference ID.

Transaction records are kept for 5 years for legal and tax compliance even after account deletion, but are anonymized so they cannot be tied back to you personally.

10

Cookies, Tokens & Sessions

On the mobile app, we use authentication tokens stored in your device's secure storage instead of cookies. These tokens are what keep you signed in between app launches.

If you use the web version of Teek (naijaprimetip.com), we use session cookies necessary for login. We do not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies on any platform.

11

How We Use Your Data

We use the data described above to:

  • Create and secure your account
  • Show your profile, posts, comments, and messages to other users as you intend
  • Deliver notifications and alerts to you
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized account access
  • Provide customer support when you contact us
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Improve the app (aggregated, non-personal usage only)

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use it for advertising.

12

Sharing Your Data

We do not share your personal data except:

  • With other users — when you post content, comment, send a message, or follow someone, that action is visible to the intended recipient(s)
  • With service providers — hosting, email delivery, and payment processing, under contracts that prevent them from using your data for their own purposes
  • For legal compliance — if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of Teek or its users
  • In a business transfer — if Teek is sold or merged, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction, and you will be notified
13

Third-Party Services

Teek uses the following third-party services to operate the social platform:

  • Payment processor (Flutterwave or similar) for wallet top-up and withdrawals — subject to their own privacy policies
  • Email delivery for verification codes, password resets, and new-login alerts
  • Hosting for our API servers and database

We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any other tracking/analytics SDK. There is no advertising SDK in Teek.

14

Data Security

We take security seriously. Specifically:

  • Passwords are hashed with industry-standard algorithms and are never stored in plain text
  • All communication between the app and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Authentication tokens are stored securely on your device and transmitted only over HTTPS
  • New-device logins are flagged and sent to your email
  • Database access is restricted to necessary personnel

No system is 100% secure. If you suspect unauthorized access, change your password immediately and contact us at the email in Section 22.

15

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Specific retention windows:

  • Account data, posts, comments, messages, followers — while your account exists, plus 30 days after deletion request
  • Transaction records — 5 years after deletion (anonymized, for tax/legal compliance)
  • Abuse reports and moderation logs — 90 days after resolution
  • Server logs (IP, request path) — 30 days

Data in the offline tools is under your control and is deleted the moment you delete the item or uninstall the app.

16

Your Rights

Under GDPR, CCPA, Nigeria's NDPR, and similar regulations, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data
  • Correct — update any inaccurate data (most of it you can edit yourself in the app)
  • Delete — request deletion of your account and data (see Section 17)
  • Port — receive a machine-readable copy of your data
  • Object / restrict — ask us to limit how we use your data
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent

To exercise any right, email us at the address in Section 22. We will respond within 30 days.

17

Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time directly from the app:

  • Open the app and sign in
  • Tap your profile icon → Delete Account
  • Type DELETE and enter your password
  • Tap Delete My Account

Deletion is scheduled for 30 days. If you log back in within that window, the deletion is cancelled and your account is fully restored. After 30 days, all your personal data (profile, posts, comments, messages, followers, notifications, social links) is permanently removed. Transaction records remain anonymized for 5 years for legal compliance.

Full details: teek-delete-account

18

Content Moderation

To keep Teek safe, we may review content (posts, messages, profiles) that is reported as abusive, illegal, or in violation of our Terms of Service. Moderation is performed by trained staff and is limited to what is necessary to respond to a specific report.

If you post content that violates our terms or the law, we may remove the content and/or suspend your account. Repeated violations will result in permanent account removal.

19

Children's Privacy

Teek is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we learn that a user is under 13, we will delete the account and all associated data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us immediately.

20

International Users

Our servers are located in Nigeria. If you are accessing Teek from outside Nigeria, your data will be transferred to and processed in Nigeria, which may have different data protection laws than your country. By using Teek, you consent to this transfer.

For users in the EU/EEA, we rely on standard contractual clauses and your explicit consent for this cross-border transfer.
