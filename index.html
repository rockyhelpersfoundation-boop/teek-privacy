Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Teek Profile is 100% offline. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal information. Everything you create stays on your device.

1. Overview

Teek Profile is built with a privacy-first philosophy. The app runs entirely offline on your device. We do not have servers, databases, or cloud storage. We cannot see your data even if we wanted to.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Teek Profile does not collect: names, emails, phone numbers, photos, work experience, education, skills, location, contacts, PINs, device identifiers, analytics, or any data transmitted over the internet.

3. Data Storage — 100% On Your Device

All information is stored locally using an encrypted SQLite database. This includes profile information, work history, education, skills, contact details, photos, and your 4-digit PIN (hashed with SHA-256).

4. Photo Access

Photo gallery access is used only to select a profile picture. The selected image is copied into the app's private storage and never uploaded anywhere.

5. Camera Access

The camera is used only to scan QR codes. Camera frames are processed locally and never recorded, stored, or transmitted.

6. PDF & QR Sharing

When you share a PDF or QR code, you control exactly what is included. Nothing is uploaded. Sharing happens through your device's native share sheet.

7. Security Features

Teek Profile includes an optional PIN lock (SHA-256 hashed), screenshot blocking, and app-hidden-from-recents for enhanced security.

8. Third-Party Services

Teek Profile does NOT use Google Analytics, Firebase, Facebook SDK, or any advertising or tracking tools.

9. Internet Permission

Teek Profile does not request the Android INTERNET permission. It cannot transmit your data anywhere, even accidentally.

10. Children's Privacy

Compliant with COPPA. Teek Profile collects no data at all.

11. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because we collect no data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. You have full control by default.

12. Data Deletion

Delete individual profiles in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all data. There is no cloud backup to worry about.

13. Changes to This Policy

Updates will appear in new app versions with a revised date.
