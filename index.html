Overview

Teek is built with a privacy-first philosophy. The app runs entirely offline on your device. We do not have servers, databases, or cloud storage. We cannot see your data even if we wanted to.

2

Information We Do NOT Collect

Teek does not collect, request, or store any of the following:

  • Names, emails, or phone numbers
  • Profile photos or any images you upload
  • Work experience, education, or skills
  • Your notes, courses, sections, or reading activity
  • Your recipes, menus, or cost sheets
  • Your scripts, shot lists, or call sheets
  • Your code snippets or dev projects
  • Your legal cases, clients, or generated drafts
  • Your business clients, deals, proposals, or invoices
  • Your music library, playlists, or listening history
  • Your photos, videos, or camera roll content
  • Your imported files, downloads, or exports
  • Your assignments or study data
  • Your location or GPS data
  • Your contacts or phone book
  • Your PIN or any security credentials
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or telemetry
  • Any data transmitted over the internet
3

Data Storage — 100% On Your Device

All information you enter is stored locally inside your device's private app storage using a private SQLite database. This includes:

  • Profile information (names, titles, bios, photos)
  • Work experience, education, and skills
  • Recommendations, certifications, projects, and languages
  • Notes you write
  • Courses and sections you create
  • Recipes, menus, and cost sheets
  • Scripts, shot lists, and call sheets
  • Code snippets and dev projects
  • Legal cases, clients, and generated drafts
  • Business clients, deals, proposals, and invoices
  • Firm branding (if you set it for legal or business exports)
  • Assignments and study data
  • Favourited photos and videos
  • Imported music and audio files
  • Imported files and downloaded content
  • Reading time tracked while you are inside a section
  • Your 4-digit PIN (stored as a SHA-256 hash, never in plain text)

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to share it (e.g., exporting a PDF or showing a QR code).

4

Workspaces

Teek adapts itself to your profession. You can type your profession (e.g., "Chef", "Software Engineer", "Lawyer", "Film Director", "Business Owner", "Student") and Teek organizes your workspace with the right tools for your work.

  • Your profession is stored only on your device
  • It is never transmitted anywhere
  • You can change it at any time in Settings
  • The workspace is a purely local layout — no data collection is involved
5

Code Preview

Teek includes an offline code preview for HTML, CSS, JavaScript, SVG, and Markdown snippets. When you tap "Preview":

  • The code is rendered inside a sandboxed view (WebView) on your device
  • No internet connection is used or required
  • No code is uploaded, transmitted, or executed on any server
  • Your code never leaves your device
  • The preview is generated entirely locally and stored only in your device's temporary memory
6

Legal Drafting

The Legal workspace includes a guided drafting assistant with 40 document templates (client briefs, letters of demand, statements of claim, contracts, etc.).

  • All templates and generated drafts are stored only on your device
  • Draft generation happens entirely offline — no AI service, no internet, no server
  • Your firm branding (name, address, contact) is stored locally and used only in the PDFs you export
  • No legal content is ever transmitted anywhere
7

Business Hub

The Business workspace includes a client management system with four tools:

  • Clients — company, contact person, email, phone, address, industry, notes
  • Deals — pipeline tracking with stages (Lead, Contacted, Proposal, Negotiation, Won, Lost), value, expected close date
  • Proposals — title, client, body, amount, status, validity period
  • Invoices — invoice number, client, line items with quantities and prices, total, status, due date

All business data is stored exclusively on your device. When you export a proposal or an invoice as a PDF:

  • The PDF is generated entirely on your device
  • Your firm branding is applied only at the moment of export
  • Nothing is uploaded to any server
  • Sharing happens through your device's native share sheet
8

Photo Gallery

Teek includes an in-app photo gallery that displays the photos already on your device. When you open the gallery:

  • Teek requests permission to read your photos
  • Photos are read-only and displayed inside the app
  • You can favourite photos; the list of favourites is stored only on your device
  • Photos are never uploaded, transmitted, or modified
  • You can revoke photo access at any time in your device settings

Teek does not access your camera roll for any other purpose.

9

Music Player

Teek includes an in-app music library and player. You import audio files into Teek, and the app can play them, show album art, and continue playback in the background with lock screen controls.

  • Imported audio files are copied into Teek's private storage on your device
  • Playback history is not tracked or transmitted
  • Your favourites and playlists are stored only on your device
  • Metadata (title, artist, album, cover art) is read directly from your files
  • No audio is ever uploaded, streamed, or transmitted
10

Videos

Teek includes an in-app video browser that lists the videos already on your device. When you tap a video, it plays in your device's native video player.

  • Videos are read-only and never uploaded or modified
  • Video thumbnails are generated locally on your device
  • Your favourites are stored only on your device
  • You can revoke video access at any time in your device settings
11

File Manager

Teek includes a file manager that organizes files inside Teek's own storage — Downloads, Exports, Attachments, and Recycle Bin.

  • Files you import into Teek are copied into Teek's private storage on your device
  • Files you delete are moved to the Recycle Bin (soft delete) and can be restored
  • Files you empty from the Recycle Bin are permanently deleted
  • PDFs you export are saved in Teek's Exports folder
  • Nothing is uploaded to any server — sharing happens via your device's share sheet

Teek does not access files outside its own private storage except through the system file picker, and only when you explicitly choose a file to import.

12

Global Search

Teek includes a global search that finds content across everything in the app — notes, profiles, courses, recipes, legal cases, business clients, and files.

  • Search runs entirely on your device — no queries are sent anywhere
  • Search indexes are kept in memory and are never uploaded
  • There is no search history transmitted, and no analytics
  • Recent items shown on the search screen are read directly from your local database
13

Reading Time Tracking

When you are inside a section (a page of a course), Teek measures how long you spend reading. This is used to show you your reading progress and total study time.

  • This data is stored only on your device
  • It is never transmitted anywhere
  • Reading pauses automatically when you leave the app
  • You can delete it by deleting the section or course
14

PDF & QR Sharing

You may choose to share your profile, a legal draft, a business proposal, or an invoice as a PDF. You may also share your profile as a QR code. When you do:

  • The PDF file is generated entirely on your device
  • The QR code contains only the information you have chosen to make public
  • You control exactly what is included
  • Nothing is uploaded to any server
  • Sharing happens through your device's native share sheet (WhatsApp, Email, Bluetooth, etc.)

Once you share a file, its privacy is determined by the app or service you share it with.

15

Security Features & PIN

Teek includes an optional 4-digit PIN lock. When enabled:

  • The PIN is hashed using SHA-256 before storage
  • The original PIN is never saved anywhere
  • The app locks when opened after being fully closed
  • Only a correct PIN unlocks your data

This means someone who gains physical access to your unlocked device still cannot easily view your Teek data.

16

Drafts & Auto-Save

While you are writing a note, creating a profile, a course, a recipe, a script, a snippet, a project, a legal case, a legal draft, a business client, deal, proposal, or invoice, Teek saves your work-in-progress as a draft on your device. This protects you from losing content if the app is closed unexpectedly.

  • Drafts are stored only on your device
  • Drafts are cleared automatically when you save
  • You can discard a draft at any time from the dashboard
  • Drafts are never transmitted anywhere
17

Third-Party Services

Teek does NOT use any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services. There are no SDKs from Google Analytics, Firebase, Facebook, or any other tracking provider.

18

Internet Permission

Teek does not request the Android INTERNET permission. This is a technical guarantee that the app cannot transmit your data anywhere — even accidentally. The app works fully in airplane mode.

The code preview feature runs entirely offline through the device's built-in WebView engine. No internet connection is required or used.

19

Children's Privacy

Teek does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. Because the app collects no data at all, it is compliant with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and similar regulations worldwide.

20

Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because Teek does not collect or process any personal data on our servers, there is no data for us to access, correct, or delete. All data exists solely on your device and is fully under your control. You can:

  • Delete any profile, note, course, recipe, script, snippet, legal case, legal draft, business client, deal, proposal, or invoice at any time
  • Export your profile, legal drafts, business proposals, or invoices as PDF at any time
  • Empty the Recycle Bin to permanently delete imported files
  • Uninstall the app to remove all data

Under GDPR, CCPA, and similar regulations, you have the right to access, correct, and delete your personal data — and Teek gives you those rights by default, permanently.

21

Data Deletion

To delete all Teek data:

  • Delete individual items inside the app (profiles, notes, courses, recipes, scripts, snippets, legal cases, legal drafts, business clients, deals, proposals, invoices)
  • Discard any pending drafts from the dashboard
  • Empty the Recycle Bin from the Files screen
  • Or uninstall the app from your device

Uninstalling removes the app's private database and all associated files (including imported music, photos, videos, and exported PDFs). There is no cloud backup to delete because there is no cloud.
