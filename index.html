Overview

Teek is built with a privacy-first philosophy. The app runs entirely offline on your device. We do not have servers, databases, or cloud storage. We cannot see your data even if we wanted to.

2

Information We Do NOT Collect

Teek does not collect, request, or store any of the following:

  • Names, emails, or phone numbers
  • Profile photos or any images you upload
  • Work experience, education, or skills
  • Location or GPS data
  • Contacts or phone book access
  • PINs or security credentials
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or telemetry
  • Any data transmitted over the internet
3

Data Storage — 100% On Your Device

All information you enter is stored locally using an encrypted SQLite database inside your device's private app storage. This includes profile information, work history, education, skills, contact details, photos, and your 4-digit PIN — which is hashed with SHA-256 and never stored in plain text.

4

Photo Access

Photo gallery access is used solely to select a profile picture. The selected image is copied into the app's private storage and is never uploaded, shared, or transmitted anywhere.

5

Camera Access

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a QR code. Camera frames are processed entirely on your device and are never recorded, stored, or transmitted.

6

PDF & QR Sharing

When you share a PDF or QR code, you have complete control over what is included. Nothing is uploaded to any server. Sharing happens through your device's native share sheet — whether it's WhatsApp, email, Bluetooth, or another app you choose.

7

Security Features

Teek includes an optional 4-digit PIN lock. Your PIN is hashed with SHA-256 before storage, screenshots are blocked inside the app, and the app is hidden from the recent apps list. Your profiles stay protected even if someone gains access to your unlocked phone.

8

Third-Party Services

Teek does NOT use Google Analytics, Firebase, Facebook SDK, or any advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting tools. There are no third-party SDKs in this app that transmit your data anywhere.

9

Internet Permission

Teek does not request the Android INTERNET permission. This is a technical guarantee that the app cannot transmit your data anywhere — even accidentally, even if it wanted to.

10

Children's Privacy

Teek is fully compliant with COPPA and similar regulations worldwide. Because the app collects no data at all, it is safe for users of any age.

11

Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because we collect no data on any server, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. You have complete control of your information by default — permanently and unconditionally.

12

Data Deletion

To delete all Teek data, simply delete individual profiles inside the app, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the app's private database and all associated files. There is no cloud backup to worry about — because there is no cloud.

13

Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this privacy policy, the new version will be included in the next app update and will show a revised "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
